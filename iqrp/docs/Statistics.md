# Statistics Engine

Descriptive stats, hypothesis tests, correlation, covariance, confidence
intervals, and information theory — `iqrp.app.math.statistics`.

## Descriptive

Mean, median, mode, variance, std, MAD, quantiles/percentiles, moments,
skewness, kurtosis, `summarize()`.

## Hypothesis tests

Student / Welch t, ANOVA, Chi-square, KS, Mann-Whitney, Wilcoxon, Shapiro-Wilk,
Jarque-Bera, ADF, KPSS.

## Correlation & covariance

Pearson, Spearman, Kendall, distance correlation, cross / rolling correlation,
empirical and shrunk covariance.

## Confidence intervals

Normal, bootstrap, Wilson score, Bayesian Beta credible intervals.

## Information theory

Entropy, cross-entropy, KL, Jensen-Shannon, mutual information, conditional
entropy, information gain, empirical estimators.
